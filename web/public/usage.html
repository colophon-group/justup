About this preview

Justup is operated by Viktor Shcherbakov and is being prepared as a temporary web preview. Features and availability may change. This draft is not an approved set of usage conditions.

Access and payments

The temporary preview does not collect payments. Access settings still require review; this draft does not grant a subscription, trial or legacy-account benefit.

Search, coverage and uncertainty

When AI is used to interpret a search or match a listing, it can misunderstand criteria, omit a useful result or include an unsuitable one. Check the employer’s posting before making a decision.

Coverage is limited to the company pages being tracked. It is not a complete view of the job market. “First seen” means when collection first observed the posting, not necessarily when the employer published it. Monitoring can have gaps, and a role may close before the next check.

Company pages and applications

Source links lead to third-party pages with their own terms and privacy notices. Review the employer’s current description and apply there. A listing or hiring trend is not a guarantee of a vacancy, an interview, an offer or completed hiring.

Requesting a company

Company discovery can identify the wrong organisation. Check the proposed company before confirming it. A confirmed request does not guarantee successful collection or a completion date. Any priority handling must match the access actually enabled for the preview.

Using the service

Do not submit information you are not entitled to share, impersonate another person, or interfere with other people’s access or the service. Do not treat generated matches or hiring statistics as verified advice.

Questions and problem reports

For questions or to report an incorrect match, an incorrect company identity, a broken source link or an access problem, email business@colophon-group.org.
