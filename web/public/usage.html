About this preview

Justup is operated by Viktor Shcherbakov and is being prepared as a temporary web preview. This draft describes the implemented local behavior and prepared features; it is not an approved set of usage conditions or proof of a deployed service.

Access and availability

The temporary preview does not collect payments and does not grant a subscription, trial or legacy-account benefit. Accounts use an invitation, username and password. The prepared account limit is 30, with an account deadline 30 days after registration; actual deployed settings still need review.

Availability is limited and features can be paused. Models remain disabled until execution, data handling and quality checks pass. Live updates, followed/viewed state, posting-language preferences, request priority, Hiring Wire and billing are unavailable in this first preview.

Fresh listings, limited coverage

The preview uses a bounded imported snapshot of public Jobseek listings, not continuous monitoring of every company. It is not a complete view of the job market.

Results are always newest first and limited to the last 14 days. “First seen” means when collection first observed the posting, not necessarily when the employer published it. Visitors can access listings first seen between 48 hours and 14 days ago; signed-in testers can access the eligible last-14-day slice.

Old, inactive or unverified listings are not shown. Missing or stale imported evidence pauses results with a retry action instead of reporting that there are no matches. A role can still close before the next source check.

Interpreting a search

When enabled, AI can misunderstand criteria, omit a useful result or include an unsuitable one. Ordinary filters run before any extra model-based matching. Check the employer’s posting before making a decision.

A failed interpretation keeps the last accepted search. A partial search with unmet requirements needs your explicit confirmation before those criteria are accepted. Editing your search text changes the request; it is not a conversation with a recruiter.

Company pages and requests

Company pages show only available imported identity and source evidence. They do not establish a company’s location, hiring performance or continuous monitoring.

Your submitted identity request and its task are private to your account. Known imported companies need no AI research. If company verification is enabled, unfamiliar or ambiguous input can require public-site evidence or clarification. Check the exact company preview before confirming it.

Confirmation records that identity and requested scope. It does not start a crawler, follow the company, grant priority or guarantee successful collection or a completion date.

Applications and responsible use

Source links lead to third-party pages with their own terms and privacy notices. Review the employer’s current description and apply there. A listing is not a guarantee of a vacancy, interview, offer or completed hiring.

Do not submit information you are not entitled to share, impersonate another person or interfere with other people’s access. Keep sensitive personal information out of searches and company requests. Do not treat generated matches as verified advice.

Questions and problem reports

For questions or to report an incorrect match, an incorrect company identity, a broken source link or an access problem, email business@colophon-group.org.
