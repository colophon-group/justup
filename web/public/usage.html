About Justup

Justup is operated by Viktor Shcherbakov. It helps you find recent roles from company career pages and inspect the sources behind the results. These usage conditions describe the service; the temporary deployment’s access and availability restrictions are set out separately below.

Temporary deployment access

The temporary deployment is invite-only, does not collect payments and does not grant a subscription, trial or legacy-account benefit. Accounts use a username and password. Accounts remain until you delete them unless an explicitly disclosed temporary account deadline applies. Registration capacity, any temporary account deadline and the deployment expiry must be verified and disclosed before invitations are issued.

Availability depends on verified source data and enabled capabilities. Models remain disabled until execution, data handling and quality checks pass. Live updates, followed/viewed state, posting-language preferences, request priority, Hiring Wire and billing are not currently available. A temporary deployment is not evidence that these product features are complete or approved.

Fresh listings, limited coverage

Coverage is limited to the companies and career-page sources shown in the catalogue. The current deployment uses a bounded imported snapshot of public Jobseek listings, not continuous monitoring of every company. It is not a complete view of the job market.

Results are always newest first and limited to the last 14 days. “First seen” means when collection first observed the posting, not necessarily when the employer published it. Visitors can access listings first seen between 48 hours and 14 days ago; signed-in accounts can access the eligible last-14-day slice.

Old, inactive or unverified listings are not shown. Missing or stale imported evidence pauses results with a retry action instead of reporting that there are no matches. A role can still close before the next source check.

Interpreting a search

When enabled, AI can misunderstand criteria, omit a useful result or include an unsuitable one. Ordinary filters run before any extra model-based matching. Check the employer’s posting before making a decision.

A failed interpretation keeps the last accepted search. A partial search with unmet requirements needs your explicit confirmation before those criteria are accepted. Editing your search text changes the request; it is not a conversation with a recruiter.

Company pages and requests

Company pages show only available imported identity and source evidence. They do not establish a company’s location, hiring performance or continuous monitoring.

Your submitted identity request and its task are private to your account. Known imported companies need no AI research. If company verification is enabled, unfamiliar or ambiguous input can require public-site evidence or clarification. Check the exact proposed company before confirming it.

Confirmation records that identity and requested scope. The current request service does not start career-page collection, follow the company or grant priority. A confirmed identity is not evidence of monitored sources and does not guarantee successful collection or a completion date.

Applications and responsible use

Source links lead to third-party pages with their own terms and privacy notices. Review the employer’s current description and apply there. A listing is not a guarantee of a vacancy, interview, offer or completed hiring.

Do not submit information you are not entitled to share, impersonate another person or interfere with other people’s access. Keep sensitive personal information out of searches and company requests. Do not treat generated matches as verified advice.

Questions and problem reports

For questions or to report an incorrect match, an incorrect company identity, a broken source link or an access problem, email business@colophon-group.org.
