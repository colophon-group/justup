Who runs Justup?

Justup is operated by Viktor Shcherbakov. For privacy questions or requests about your data, email business@colophon-group.org.

If you contact us by email, we receive your message and reply address to answer your request. The mailbox provider and retention settings still need review.

Accounts and private records

Account access uses a local username and password. Registration does not ask for an email address. The service currently has no Google login, resume upload or payment collection. Access restrictions for the temporary deployment are listed below.

The server checks your password and stores a salted password hash. It stores hashes of random session tokens rather than the tokens themselves. Sessions expire after at most seven days. Accounts are retained until you delete them unless an explicitly disclosed temporary account deadline is configured; a session cannot outlive such a deadline. Current deployment settings and cleanup limits are listed below.

Your account, saved searches, company requests and their background tasks are private account records. The server checks ownership and protects changes against cross-site requests. Public job and company records are separate from these private records.

Searches and AI

When search interpretation is enabled, OpenAI receives your search text, relevant prior accepted query and criteria, supported filter values and validation feedback to interpret your requirements. Model-backed features remain disabled until execution, data handling and quality checks are complete.

If ordinary filters cannot express a requirement and AI matching is available, TypeSafe’s JEV service receives that remaining condition and eligible public posting text and metadata to check the match. The application does not add your Justup account identifier, username, password, session token or billing identifiers to these model inputs.

What you type is still part of the input. Do not put sensitive personal information in a search or company request. Provider retention, training use, processing countries and transfer arrangements still require verification before these features can be enabled.

Company requests

A submitted request stores the company name or website, any supplied scope, the proposed identity and confirmation state under your account. Opening a prefilled form does not submit it. Known imported companies can be identified without an AI call.

When company verification is enabled, an unfamiliar company can require a limited amount of public official-site evidence. Your company input and that evidence go to OpenAI. Public websites contacted by the server can receive ordinary request metadata. An uncertain identity must remain unresolved or ask for clarification; you confirm the exact proposed company before it is recorded.

Company discovery is being configured to use OpenAI’s web search and browsing tools. Native-web discovery remains disabled until its search and browsing inputs, default request metadata and provider retention are verified and covered by reviewed notices.

A confirmed request does not start career-page collection, follow a company or establish that Justup monitors it.

Cookies, browser state and diagnostics

The HTTPS service uses essential host-only session and request-protection cookies, marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Cookie lifetimes are listed below; their deployed values still need to be checked.

The application keeps sensitive account, query, draft and request state in memory or on the server, not in browser localStorage. It includes no analytics or advertising tracker. This does not mean the hosting services keep no access or security logs.

Application diagnostics are restricted to request identifiers, route categories, status, duration, redacted error codes and bounded token/cost counts. They exclude raw searches, request bodies, usernames, passwords, tokens and IP addresses. Network-derived information is also used in memory to limit abusive request rates. Local diagnostic retention is bounded by the deployment settings below; actual host cleanup and hosting-layer logging remain under review.

Hosting and outside websites

Cloudflare is intended to deliver the website and forward its API requests, including account-form submissions and cookies, to the isolated Hetzner application and database host in Nuremberg, Germany. OpenAI and TypeSafe are the intended AI recipients described above. Their processing locations, provider-side retention and applicable transfer arrangements are not yet verified.

The current imported job and company records contain no remote logo URLs. Employer and source links open outside Justup when you choose them. Those websites receive your web request and apply their own privacy notices.

Retention and deletion

Private records are removed when their retention deadline is reached through cleanup while the API is running. An access deadline does not prove physical deletion at that moment if the service is stopped. Recovery pruning runs after a successful backup or an explicit cleanup command; there is no automatic backup schedule. Current lifetimes and recovery limits are listed below and must be verified before approval.

Password-confirmed account deletion removes the current account, sessions and its private searches, requests, tasks and matching data. Private deletion invalidates and purges recovery backups, and restoring a database revokes its sessions. These are logical and file-level controls: immediate secure erasure of storage remnants or copies already held by a provider is not promised.

Use the contact above for questions about access, correction or deletion, including when an in-app control is unavailable.

Temporary deployment settings

The isolated preview uses invitation access. Its registration capacity, any temporary account deadline and deployment expiry must be verified and disclosed before invitations are issued. Accounts have no automatic expiry by default. A session lasts up to seven days, and a signed-out request-protection cookie lasts one hour.

Local matching decisions and diagnostic logs have seven-day limits. Recovery tooling permits at most two SQLite backups, with a seven-day usable backup age. These settings describe the temporary deployment, not a subscription or a permanent account-expiry promise. Any change to access, retention or recipients requires review of these notices before activation.

What still needs review?

Before approval, the fixed deployment must confirm cookie values, ownership and deletion behavior, cleanup and backup pruning, hosting/access/security logs, contact-mailbox handling, and each provider’s actual metadata, retention, processing locations and transfer arrangements. Model isolation and quality evidence and the final operator review are also outstanding. This draft is not an approved notice for active accounts or model use.
