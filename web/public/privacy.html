Identity and sign-in

The login provider, exact permissions and account identifiers need to be confirmed. This draft does not claim that Google sign-in or any particular Google permission is active.

Search and AI matching

The reviewed notice must identify which search text and profile details are sent to an AI service, why they are needed, which provider receives them and how that provider handles them. These fields and settings are not yet confirmed. Do not include sensitive personal details in a search.

Company requests

The reviewed notice must explain what a request records, who can see it, how it is associated with an account and which services receive it. Request visibility and retention have not yet been confirmed.

Cookies, storage, analytics and logs

The actual session cookies, browser storage, analytics and hosting or application logs must be inventoried, including their purpose and duration. This draft makes no claim that visiting the preview is free of logging or storage.

Recipients and processing locations

The hosting, identity, AI and other processors must be named. Processing countries and any international transfers, protection arrangements and safeguards still need confirmation.

Retention, deletion and privacy requests

The reviewed notice must state how long account, search, request and log data are kept, including provider copies and backups. It must describe the deletion controls that actually work, any exceptions, and how to request access, correction or deletion. No deletion timing or outcome is promised by this draft.
