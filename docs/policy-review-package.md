# Justup policy review package

**Status: internal proposal, 1 November 2026. Do not publish this file as a policy or submit it to a provider.** The served notices remain `product-draft-5`, unapproved, with no effective date. Their current English source is [`web/policies/content.ts`](../web/policies/content.ts); German, French, Italian and Spanish have corresponding drafts. This package owns no approval flag. Merge selected language into those source notices only after the actual feature and deployment inventory is fixed, then review every admitted language and its exact served bytes. Remove this temporary package after its accepted decisions and wording are reflected in the served notices and issue [#34](https://github.com/colophon-group/justup/issues/34).

The aim is one truthful website notice pair that can be extended for a selected sign-in or payment provider. A generic claim that every possible provider is active would mislead users and fail provider review. The clauses below are therefore divided into **core wording** and **activation clauses**. Brackets mark facts or decisions that must be resolved; they are not publishable text. The clauses are proposals for operator and legal review, not a compliance finding.

Operator answers received in chat: Viktor Shcherbakov will operate Justup **personally**, rather than through a registered company; the intended audience and paid offer are **worldwide**, subject to provider availability and applicable restrictions. A selected merchant-of-record arrangement may identify a different transaction seller on checkout and receipts; confirm that role before final payment copy. The product is a general-audience job service with **no arbitrary 18+ cutoff**. The operator proposed **16+ as a practical account minimum**; public job browsing can remain available without an age gate. This is a proposed rule pending final product/legal review, not a claim that 16 resolves every country's age, guardian or purchase requirements. Final app declarations must match the actual intended audience. Collect only age information needed to apply the chosen rule. [FTC guidance on general-audience services](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions), [EDPB child-consent context](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en), [Google Play audience declarations](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159).

The operator selected local username/password **and Google, Apple, Microsoft and LinkedIn sign-in for first release**. None of the four external login routes is active in the current served policy; implementation, scopes, provider review, account linking and approved notice copy remain prerequisites. This selection does not itself authorize provider provisioning, live data processing or a public login claim.

The sign-in experience should stay simple: one provider action creates a Justup account or opens the account already bound to that provider subject. It must not require a user to create a local password first. A signed-in user can link another method later in settings. A matching email address alone must not silently merge separate accounts; a mistaken second account needs a clear recovery or linking path. Keep identity proof for free-period eligibility separate from the convenience of sign-in.

The operator chose **verified control of an email address** as sufficient to start the ordinary free week, independent of the account's sign-in method. Registration remains one step; verification happens only when the user chooses to activate free access. The same verified address must not support overlapping claims on active accounts. This does not establish a unique real-world person: distinct addresses can qualify separately, and that limitation is accepted for the simple launch flow. Do not describe this as identity-document or unique-person verification.

The operator chose **USD as the base currency** and **$3 per week or $10 per month including applicable indirect tax** as the proposed starting web-subscription prices. This supersedes the earlier tax-exclusive instruction. Configure an explicitly tax-inclusive price so tax is taken from the stated amount rather than added to it, and show the final payable amount before confirmation. Local-currency conversions may differ from the USD figures and must be clear before purchase. The included tax reduces net proceeds where it applies; model that effect alongside Managed Payments, processing and subscription fees. The operator intends to model the economics later and may readjust the amounts. These are operator-selected planning terms, not permission to activate live checkout: local-currency presentation, supported sales countries, refund/cancellation rules, provider eligibility, fee ceiling and final checkout review remain open. If modelling changes the amounts before or after launch, checkout, Usage copy and relevant subscription notices must be updated together before the new price applies. [Stripe Managed Payments tax behavior](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-tax-inclusive-and-tax-exclusive-pricing-with-managed-payments).

If Managed Payments is approved, the operator wants Link to handle eligible customer refund requests **without requesting case-by-case approval**. Confirm the selected account setting and its rules before activation. The public terms should direct payment and subscription refund requests to the actual Link route, without adding a voluntary refund guarantee. Stripe says it can issue refunds at its discretion in some disputes; this setting does not mean every request is refundable. [Stripe Managed Payments refund handling](https://support.stripe.com/questions/managed-payments-transaction-support-refunds-and-order-management).

The operator chose **no additional voluntary guarantees across the product**. State the actual service and paid-access commitments, and preserve all non-waivable consumer and privacy rights. Do not promise an extra refund window, uninterrupted availability, perfect AI matching, complete job coverage, continuous monitoring or hiring outcomes. Do not use this choice to deny a refund, remedy or warranty required by applicable law or the selected merchant-of-record terms.

For the longer welcome month, the operator wants **no separate Jobseek claim ceremony**. Check the verified identity associated with the Justup account against the frozen qualifying Jobseek account cohort. Jobseek currently records a user email, its verification state, and provider account identifiers for GitHub, Google and LinkedIn. The implementation should prefer an exact same-provider subject where available; where that is unavailable, it may use the independently verified mailbox already required for free-period activation against the frozen Jobseek account email. A typed address, display name or unverified provider email is insufficient. Keep raw historical email and provider tokens out of the Justup entitlement ledger; retain only the keyed evidence required while the account exists. A mismatch receives ordinary eligibility without an extra Jobseek code flow. Verify the frozen cohort, account dates and matching rules before activation.

## Proposed Privacy notice: core wording

### Who we are and how to contact us

Justup is a job discovery service operated by **Viktor Shcherbakov personally**. Contact **business@colophon-group.org** to ask about your personal data, request access or correction, seek deletion, or report a privacy concern. [Confirm any business address or additional contact details required for countries served.] If you email us, we receive your reply address and the contents of your message. [Name the mailbox provider, processing country, and retention rule.]

### What we handle and why

We process information you supply to operate the features you use: account credentials and sessions; searches, saved criteria and drafts; optional language preferences, followed companies and source-opening markers; company names, links or scope you submit for identity requests; and messages you send to support. The server associates private saved work with an account or a temporary visitor owner. Public job and company records are distinct from your private records. Public browsing alone does not create a saved visitor workspace. [Verify the enabled feature set and the exact visitor creation and transfer behavior on the deployed service.]

The site uses essential session and request-protection cookies. An optional language cookie records only a manually chosen interface language. The application does not place search text or account records in browser localStorage and currently has no advertising tracker. Network and hosting providers may nevertheless process request and security information. Our application diagnostics are designed to record route category, status, request ID, duration, safe error code and bounded usage counts, without raw searches, passwords or tokens. [Read back actual cookie names, lifetimes, host logs, analytics, crash tools and retention before approval.]

When enabled, OpenAI receives the search text you enter, bounded prior accepted criteria, supported filter values and validation feedback to interpret your request. When ordinary filters cannot express a requirement, TypeSafe's JEV service can receive that remaining condition and eligible public posting evidence to assess matches. The application does not add account IDs, passwords, session tokens or billing identifiers to those model inputs; your own text may contain personal information. Company discovery may send your submitted company name, domain, link or scope and public evidence to OpenAI, and the server may contact outside websites. [Confirm the enabled model profiles, exact tools and fields, provider settings, retention, training use, location and transfers. Disable a profile whose facts cannot be disclosed accurately.]

Where enabled, a signed-in user may supply an email address to prove control of that mailbox. Resend then receives the recipient address, a verification message and a delivery reference. This is separate from ordinary username/password sign-in, which asks for no email. Sending through Ireland does not mean Resend stores data there: Resend states that message content, delivery logs, webhook payloads and account records are stored in the United States. [Verify the chosen Resend account, terms, retention and transfer mechanism before activation.]

If the site offers a paid web subscription, the selected payment service receives the information needed to create and manage checkout, payment, receipts, subscription state, cancellation, refunds and fraud or dispute handling. Justup retains only the account-linked customer, subscription and event identifiers needed to enforce access and reconcile changes. We do not send search text or job history to the payment service. [Name the actual legal seller/merchant of record, provider, data fields, countries, retention, and whether payment card data reaches Justup's systems. This paragraph is conditional and is absent from the current served draft.]

### Recipients, locations and outside links

Cloudflare is intended to deliver the website and route API requests to the application and database on Hetzner in Nuremberg, Germany. OpenAI, TypeSafe and conditional Resend receive only the data described for their enabled feature. [Add the verified support-mailbox provider, active identity provider, payment provider, subprocessors, processing countries and transfer protections.] Employer and other source links take you to independent websites when you choose to open them; those sites receive your request under their own notices. Imported Justup records currently provide no remote logo URL; reassess this statement before enabling external logo delivery.

### Retention and your choices

Account records remain until deletion unless a clearly disclosed temporary account deadline applies. Sessions have a configurable fixed lifetime, seven days by default. Temporary visitor work has a configurable fixed lifetime, 24 hours by default. Matching decisions, application diagnostics and conditional private journey/model-execution records have seven-day local limits. Explicitly reviewed synthetic or redacted cases may have a longer, bounded period; live user searches are not automatically copied into those cases. Backup recovery is limited to at most two SQLite backups of no more than seven days usable age. [Verify actual configuration, running cleanup, backup pruning, support-mailbox and provider-side retention. Avoid promising physical erasure at a deadline when a host is stopped or deletion is blocked.]

An account-deletion request removes the account, sessions and associated private searches, drafts, preferences, follows, viewed markers, company requests, tasks, matching and access records through the composed deletion path. It also removes keyed identity evidence and free-period redemption records. A person who later proves eligibility again may receive a new free period. If a paid subscription is attached, the service first verifies its termination with the payment provider; the account and private records remain while termination is pending or uncertain, and deletion is not reported complete until that proof is obtained. Deletion invalidates Justup's recovery backups, but does not itself erase provider copies or guarantee immediate secure erasure of storage remnants. [Verify the complete deployed path, including pending cancellation, deletion and restore, before using this wording as a final promise. Define user-facing timing and help for an unavailable provider.] Ask us at the contact above if you need access, correction or deletion help, including when the in-product control is unavailable.

### Legal and international-processing information to complete

For each purpose above, [identify the applicable legal basis if the GDPR applies, and explain any legitimate interest or consent choice where used]. [Identify countries outside the user's country and the applicable transfer safeguards, with a way to obtain information about them.] [Add legally required rights, complaint authority, representative or data protection contact for the selected markets.] [State the minimum age or guardian rule if the product will serve minors.] These facts must be checked against the selected launch countries and provider contracts; no jurisdiction is inferred from the operator's location alone.

## Proposed Usage terms: core wording

### Service and access

Justup helps people find recent jobs from tracked company sources. Coverage is partial, listings can close or change, and “first seen” is the time a source was first observed rather than the employer's publication time. The current bounded import is not continuous monitoring. AI can misread a requirement or include or exclude a useful role. Check the employer's original posting before making decisions. Justup does not accept job applications on an employer's behalf or guarantee an interview, offer or hiring result.

Visitors and accounts without current full access receive the limited freshness slice; verified full access receives the eligible last-14-day slice under the actual service rules. Signing in alone does not grant that access. [Confirm the selected launch entitlement, invitation/capacity setting, account deadline if any, and availability of each feature.] A seven-day free period starts only after the account holder proves control of an email address and explicitly activates it. Distinct addresses may qualify separately; the same address cannot support overlapping active-account claims. A Justup account whose verified identity matches an eligible former Jobseek account receives one UTC calendar month instead, without a separate Jobseek claim step. Repeating activation on the same account does not restart either period. Account deletion erases redemption evidence, so fresh verification after re-registration may qualify again. [Do not offer these periods until the frozen-cohort match, proof path and notices are approved.]

Saved searches and company requests are private to their account or temporary visitor owner where supported. Confirm the proposed company identity before accepting it. A company request or confirmation does not promise publication, continuous monitoring, a completion date, or priority beyond the access level actually enabled. Source links lead to independent sites with their own terms. “Viewed” means a source-opening action was reported; it is not proof of an application or hire.

You must not impersonate others, submit material you lack permission to share, misuse others' data, interfere with the service, or use automated access that harms its operation. Do not submit sensitive personal information in search or company-request text. [Set an enforceable fair-use/rate policy if one will be imposed on users.] We may restrict abusive use or temporarily pause unavailable features; [state notice, appeal and any paid-service effect appropriate to the final business model]. [If the proposed 16+ account rule is adopted, state it here and make registration and purchase flows enforce the same rule without blocking ordinary public browsing. Apply any additional local purchase or guardian requirement where relevant.]

### Paid subscriptions: insert only for an active purchase route

The purchase screen and these terms must agree on the seller, the exact weekly or monthly plan price and currency, taxes, what access it unlocks, when the first charge occurs, automatic renewal, how to cancel, when cancellation takes effect, payment failure and grace, refunds and statutory consumer rights. A cardless free period must never silently become a paid subscription. [Select the live Stripe Managed Payments or other merchant arrangement and verify which entity sells, bills, refunds, handles tax and supports customers in each sales country.] [Insert the selected tax-inclusive USD prices, actual transaction seller, renewal interval, cancellation route, Link refund request path, failed-payment rule and customer-support route after final provider verification. There is no extra voluntary refund guarantee; preserve all mandatory rights and do not promise that every eligible-refund request is approved.] Do not direct a native-app user to an outside purchase route until the applicable store rules and regional entitlements have been reviewed.

### Changes, questions and legal terms

We may change the service as its coverage and features evolve. Material changes to data use, access or paid terms will be presented before they take effect where required; an already paid period and applicable consumer rights remain governed by the terms accepted for that purchase. [Set the notice period, acceptance mechanism and any migration/refund rule.] For questions, incorrect companies or broken sources, contact **business@colophon-group.org**. Justup makes no additional voluntary promise of uninterrupted availability, perfect results, complete coverage or hiring outcomes. [Set the legal contracting party, applicable law, dispute venue or consumer dispute route, and proportionate warranty/liability wording after legal review; preserve all mandatory rights and the paid access actually sold.]
