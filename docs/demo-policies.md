## Prepared model boundary — verification pending

The backend's current correction targets pinned Codex **0.159.2** with Luna and **zero native model tools**. The broker, rather than model web/code tools, performs bounded official-page fetches and supplies company input plus bounded public evidence to the model. Search interpretation supplies the query/prior configuration/capabilities/compiler feedback described above. TypeSafe/JEV remains the separate residual matching recipient.

The prepared company flow is bounded: known imported company, zero inferences; explicit website, at most two trusted official pages and one grounded call; name-only input, at most one private unverified domain proposal (512 observed tokens), followed by official-page fetch and one grounded call. The corrected path targets a combined 6,000-output-token/60-second bound, published URLs restricted to fetched evidence and name grounding in title, heading or visible text. An unverified proposal is never a public confirmed identity; lack of evidence gives honest clarification/unresolved output. Public sites contacted by this process can receive ordinary network request metadata.

This correction is not deployed or proven by the earlier model source. Do not claim native web, code-mode or hook execution, an additional search provider, successful isolation or zero provider retention. Before activation, retain evidence for the exact corrected source/runtime, advertised empty tool catalogue, dedicated UID/filesystem/network boundaries, invocation logs/cache/trace retention, cleanup after failure/crash, provider/subscription/default-account metadata, schema/evidence validation, quality, latency and budget. The public drafts keep this flow conditional while models remain off.

## Usage and public-page boundaries

The bounded imported snapshot is not continuous company monitoring. Eligibility is newest-first, last 14 days from original source first-observed time; visitors have a 48-hour delay. Missing/stale evidence pauses rather than proving completed zero. Search warnings require explicit partial-criteria confirmation, and a failed interpretation preserves the accepted revision. Company requests remain private and require exact identity confirmation; confirmation does not onboard a crawler or promise a completion date.

Billing, SSE, follows/viewed writes, posting-language preferences, request priority, Hiring Wire, trials and legacy entitlements are not active preview promises. Employer/source links leave Justup and have their own terms and privacy handling. No company headquarters or hiring-performance data is inferred.

The policy pages load only local CSS, with no JavaScript, fonts, analytics or identity-provider assets. They remain readable without an account/API and carry noindex metadata. Hosting their files can still generate hosting logs. `tests/browser/policies.spec.mjs` checks those delivery properties, draft/version/operator/contact facts, concrete data-flow copy, explicit remaining gaps, usage limits and mobile/keyboard navigation. The unreviewed-policy account journey continues to require disabled sign-in.

Run `npm run build`, then `npm run test:browser -- tests/browser/policies.spec.mjs` and the focused policy-gate journeys. These tests verify draft content and delivery only; real host/provider/deletion/browser evidence remains under [demo-release-verification](https://github.com/colophon-group/justup/issues/36).
