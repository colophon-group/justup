# Justup interactive prototype

A buildless, browser-local mockup of the Extra Orange edition. It uses 480 fictional jobs and 20 fictional companies. No API keys, build step, backend or real requests are required. Pinned D3 geometry utilities are bundled locally.

From this repository:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 4173 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory prototype
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:4173.

- Scroll the positions feed. Each additional job starts only when its slot reaches the visible viewport, at least 450 ms after the preceding reveal starts. Background candidate pages are buffered independently. Leaving the tail or backgrounding the tab stops consumption.
- Fresh visits show a compact landing page with “Fresh jobs. No paid placement.”, the working search field, three editable starting points, and four brief feature explanations: no sponsored listings, first-seen freshness, inspectable company coverage, and no recruitment agencies or aggregator/social-media reposts. The coverage link opens the catalogue; the overview heading is “Beat the endless scroll.” The landing title and meta description reflect these policies; this private prototype does not implement production indexing. The feature overview appears only with no saved searches; blank additional tabs keep a compact composer. Examples prefill without submitting. No tabs, result feed or live arrivals start until the first submission; returning visits restore existing searches and drafts.
- Search deletion opens a named confirmation dialog. Keep search or Escape cancels it; deleting an inactive search preserves the current unsent query. The last search can also be deleted; confirmation returns to the mini landing page and remains empty on reload, preserving follows, viewed jobs and company requests.
- A fixed back-to-top button appears after scrolling down. It restores focus to the top navigation and respects reduced motion.
- Companies has All companies, Followed and Requested filters. Requested replaces the standalone Requests tab and renders the shared company-row component, with text-size logos, names, company details, request status, single row separators and shared profile links.
- Position search, company search and company discovery share the cream field, typography, inset submit button, border and focus treatment. Company search filters live and supports Enter or Search; discovery uses Find company. Single-line company inputs stay compact.
- Edit the complete search text and press Enter. Submit your first search from scratch or edit an example, then create additional tabs as needed. This prototype uses a small local interpreter; it does not call a model or implement JEV.
- Click anywhere on a job row to open a clearly labelled sample company page in a new tab. The company name independently opens its catalogue profile. Viewed state is shared by all searches within the same account, including New-marker dismissal.
- Browse and search the company catalogue. It uses the same viewport-gated, 450 ms row reveal as the positions feed. Whole company rows open their profiles; the compact priority action stays independent. Read each row’s coverage status and request priority on an existing setup. Coverage states do not have separate catalogue filters. Both lists share one row component for link hit areas, hover/pressed/focus feedback, arrow targets and responsive edge insets (16 px desktop, 12 px mobile). The company section bar follows its search, matching Positions. The lists have no footers or bottom coverage panels. The company-request action remains in the catalogue header; the end-of-results status uses reserved space.
- Follow companies from catalogue rows or profiles. Follow / Following buttons remain independent of whole-row links, grouped beside the profile arrow on the right. Company and request rows omit website subtitles. The catalogue’s Followed filter combines with the company search text and updates immediately on unfollow. Enable “Only followed companies” beneath a search to intersect its criteria with your followed set. The scope is saved per tab, query drafts are preserved, and live arrivals respect the limit. With no follows, the empty state links to the catalogue. New company requests automatically follow the company. Users can unfollow without removing the request; explicit unfollows persist across reloads. Manual follows do not create requests.
- Company profiles and catalogue/request rows omit company location text; the mockup does not claim headquarters data. Job locations remain visible, and globe highlights represent hiring locations. Company profiles use a cream-and-black interactive globe without a legend or note. Scrolling over the sphere rotates it, with no zoom or automatic spin. Dragging and arrow keys also rotate; Home resets. Page scrolling works outside the sphere; vertical touch scrolling remains available. The circular projection starts over each company’s distinct hiring countries and supports all world regions. Sample companies include the Americas, Africa, Asia, Oceania and Europe; sample searches understand those countries and regions. The description follows without a dividing line. Last-check information and address-based links to every tracked career page sit under the company name, without requiring board labels; there is no status table. Career links open clearly labelled sample company pages in this demo. They link to the main positions search instead of repeating a “Latest from…” job preview. Product explanations use plain language such as “Being added” and “First seen on Justup.”
- Request a missing company, reject or accept the sample identity, and see its immediate catalogue entry and setup request under Companies → Requested. Company metadata generated here is explicitly illustrative, not verified discovery. The request headline is WANTED, with “Send us a lead” and related edition copy. Confirmation omits URLs, redundant unknown metadata and extra rules; company profiles retain tracked career-page links.
- Open Demo controls to compare Ink, Dock and Glide at 450 ms, simulate a fresh arrival, a matching gap or the actual end, and enable reduced motion. The account icon opens the Settings overlay; its temporary account selector previews signed out, Plus, free week, expired and legacy welcome-month accounts. Profiles have separate searches, follows, requests and viewed state. Only paid Plus has priority in this mockup; trial request priority remains a product decision.

- The header retains the large masthead, motto and Live edition; the small JUSTUP.WORK link is removed. The original typewriter face appears in the motto, compact controls, workflow steps, checkbox/select labels, tabs, filters and metadata. “Fresh work. Clear sources.” and “Followed” share its letterforms. Body copy, job titles and company names use sans-serif; display headings retain condensed type. Row titles, secondary headings, controls and descriptions share a size/weight scale.
- Signed-out and expired accounts preview matches older than 48 hours, still within the last 14 days. The notice shows the exact number of recent hidden matches in this finite mock dataset, scoped to the active search and followed-company limit. No overall position totals appear. Hidden results are absent from preview rows and live arrivals update only the aggregate notice. This is simulated entitlement behavior, not a security boundary: the static demo contains all fictional fixtures. In production, access and counting must be enforced by the server, with a lower-bound hidden count while JEV evaluation is incomplete.
- Simulated sign-in accepts an email, preserves the visitor’s search and resumes pending follows or company requests. No email is sent. New mock accounts receive a free week; the legacy fixture has a 30-day welcome period representing the intended calendar-month offer. Access end dates, illustrative $3 weekly/$10 monthly options, plan changes, renewal cancellation and resumption are interactive. Expiry returns to preview access while keeping saved state. No payments are collected.
- The interface detects the first supported browser language, falling back to English, with English, German, French and Spanish available in Settings. An explicit choice persists across reloads. Dates and interface labels follow the chosen locale; job titles, company descriptions and query text remain original. Reduced motion is also available in Settings.

Searches (including their followed-company scope), followed companies, viewed IDs, requests and confirmed company profiles are saved in localStorage in this browser. Demo controls can reset them. There is no authentication, subscription billing, SSE connection, actual company discovery, real crawler setup or cross-device persistence. The intended application framework remains undecided.
